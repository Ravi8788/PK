# PK Graphics - Admin Guide

Welcome to your PK Graphics portfolio website! This guide will help you manage your content through the admin dashboard.

## Getting Started

### First Time Setup

1. **Access the Admin Panel**
   - Click "Admin Login" in the footer of your website
   - Or navigate directly to `/admin/login`

2. **Create Your Admin Account**
   - Click the "Sign Up" tab
   - Enter your name, email, and password
   - Click "Create Account"
   - You'll be automatically logged in

3. **Sign In (After Account Creation)**
   - Click the "Sign In" tab
   - Enter your email and password
   - Click "Sign In"

## Managing Your Content

### Portfolio Items

Portfolio items are the photos displayed in your portfolio gallery with category filters.

**To Add a Portfolio Item:**
1. Go to the "Portfolio" tab in the admin dashboard
2. Click "Add Item"
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Title**: Name of the shoot/project
   - **Category**: Choose from Wedding Shoots, Pre-Wedding Shoots, Reels/Short Videos, or Creative/Commercial Shoots
   - **Description** (Optional): Additional details about the project
   - **Image**: Click to upload a photo from your computer
4. Click "Add Item"

**To Delete a Portfolio Item:**
- Click the "Delete" button on any portfolio item card

### Reels

Reels are vertical video cards displayed in a special section with social media integration.

**To Add a Reel:**
1. Go to the "Reels" tab
2. Click "Add Reel"
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Title**: Name of the reel
   - **Video URL**: Link to your video (YouTube, Instagram, etc.)
   - **View Count**: Display number (e.g., "100K")
   - **Thumbnail Image**: Upload a thumbnail image for the reel
4. Click "Add Reel"

**To Delete a Reel:**
- Click the "Delete" button on any reel card

### Testimonials

Client testimonials with photos and ratings.

**To Add a Testimonial:**
1. Go to the "Testimonials" tab
2. Click "Add Testimonial"
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Client Name**: Full name of the client
   - **Role/Event Type**: e.g., "Wedding Client", "Fashion Brand"
   - **Testimonial**: The review text
   - **Rating**: Click stars to select (1-5)
   - **Client Photo**: Upload a photo of the client
4. Click "Add Testimonial"

**To Delete a Testimonial:**
- Click the "Delete" button on any testimonial card

### Contact Messages

View messages submitted through your contact form.

**To View Contact Messages:**
1. Go to the "Contact Messages" tab
2. Browse all messages sorted by date
3. Each message shows:
   - Client name and email
   - Message content
   - Submission date

**Note:** Contact messages cannot be deleted from the dashboard. They are stored for your records.

## Image Upload Guidelines

- **Supported Formats**: JPG, PNG, GIF
- **Recommended Size**: 
  - Portfolio items: 1920x1280px or higher
  - Thumbnails: 1080x1920px (vertical)
  - Client photos: 500x500px or higher
- **File Size Limit**: 50MB per image

## Tips for Best Results

### Portfolio Management
- Upload high-quality images that showcase your best work
- Use descriptive titles that clients can understand
- Organize shoots by appropriate categories for easy filtering
- Regularly update with your latest work

### Testimonials
- Request permission before publishing client photos and reviews
- Use real photos when possible for authenticity
- Include specific details about what clients appreciated

### Reels
- Link to videos hosted on platforms like YouTube or Instagram
- Use eye-catching thumbnails
- Update view counts periodically to show engagement

## Security Best Practices

1. **Keep Your Login Credentials Safe**
   - Use a strong, unique password
   - Don't share your admin credentials
   - Log out when finished managing content

2. **Regular Updates**
   - Keep your portfolio fresh with recent work
   - Archive old content by deleting outdated items

3. **Backup Important Data**
   - Keep original photos and videos on your local drive
   - Save important client information separately

## Accessing Your Website

- **Public Website**: Your main domain (/)
- **Admin Login**: /admin/login
- **Admin Dashboard**: /admin (requires login)

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Log In?
- Double-check your email and password
- Make sure you've created an account first (Sign Up)
- Clear browser cache and try again

### Image Won't Upload?
- Check file size (must be under 50MB)
- Ensure format is JPG, PNG, or GIF
- Try a different browser if issue persists

### Content Not Appearing?
- Refresh the public website
- Check that you successfully added the item (look for success message)
- Make sure you're viewing the correct category filter
